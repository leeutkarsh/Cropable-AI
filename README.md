### AI-Powered Agricultural Intelligence Platform

**Cropable AI** is an AI-driven agriculture platform developed by **Team Prakrati AI** to detect crop diseases and pests, analyze field conditions, predict agricultural outcomes, and provide actionable insights for smarter crop management.

The project combines **Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Artificial Neural Networks, weather intelligence, soil analysis, and geospatial data** to move beyond simple disease detection toward comprehensive agricultural decision support.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌱 Crop Disease Detection

Uses **YOLO-based computer vision** to detect crop diseases from images and identify affected areas with confidence scores.

Currently includes support for diseases such as:

* Rice Blast
* Brown Spot
* Bacterial Blight

The detection system can be extended to additional crops and diseases.

### 🐛 Pest Detection

Identifies agricultural pests using machine-learning models and agricultural datasets, helping detect potential threats before they become larger field-level problems.

### 🧠 Hybrid Prediction System

Cropable AI combines multiple machine-learning approaches for agricultural prediction.

* **YOLO** — visual disease and pest detection
* **Random Forest / XGBoost** — structured agricultural prediction
* **ANN (Artificial Neural Network)** — hybrid predictive analysis

This allows both visual information and structured agricultural parameters to contribute to predictions.

### 🧪 Soil & Fertilizer Intelligence

Analyzes soil-related parameters such as:

* Nitrogen
* Phosphorus
* Potassium
* pH
* Soil type

The system can use these parameters to assist with fertilizer recommendations and crop-related predictions.

### 🌦️ Weather Intelligence

Integrates weather information to provide environmental context for agricultural analysis, including temperature, humidity, rainfall, and forecast conditions.

### 🗺️ Field Risk Analysis

Cropable AI is designed to identify disease and pest hotspots across agricultural fields, enabling field-level risk visualization rather than focusing only on individual plants.

### 🚨 Regional Outbreak Monitoring

Multiple farmer or field reports can be aggregated geographically to identify potential disease or pest outbreaks affecting a region.

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Farmer Reports

Farmers can submit field information that can be converted into structured reports containing:

* Crop information
* Disease/pest observations
* Location
* Environmental conditions
* Risk indicators
* AI-assisted recommendations

These reports can then be reviewed by authorized staff or agricultural managers.

### 🛰️ Satellite-to-Drone Intelligence

A planned extension of Cropable AI is a **satellite → drone → field verification** workflow.

Satellite-derived vegetation data can identify potentially abnormal zones, after which higher-resolution drone or ground imagery can be used for detailed analysis.

This is intended to make large-scale agricultural monitoring more efficient.

## 🎯 Our Goal

Most agricultural AI systems stop at:

> **"This plant has a disease."**

Cropable AI aims to go further:

> **"This is the problem, this is where it is occurring, these are the environmental factors involved, this is the potential risk, and this is what can be done next."**

The long-term vision is to build an intelligent agricultural platform capable of supporting **farmers, agricultural organizations, field managers, and decision-makers** with data-driven crop intelligence.
